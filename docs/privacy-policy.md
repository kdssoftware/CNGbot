# Privacy Policy for CNGbot

**Effective Date:** 28th of August 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how CNGbot collects, stores, and protects your data. The Bot is hosted on a private server (operated by Contabo) located in Germany. As the operator is a resident of Belgium, this policy is designed to comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## 2. Information We Collect

To provide its functionality, the Bot collects and processes the following data:

* **Discord Data:** User IDs, Discord usernames, server (Guild) IDs, role IDs, and channel IDs.
* **EVE Online Data (via ESI API):** Character IDs, Corporation IDs, Alliance IDs, in-game standings, calendar events, and EVE Mail content (only when mail forwarding or 2FA features are explicitly configured).
* **Authentication Data:** SeAT registration statuses and OAuth refresh tokens necessary to maintain active ESI connections.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly to operate the Bot's features within your server:

* Synchronizing Discord roles with EVE Online entities.
* Forwarding relevant in-game communications and calendar events to designated Discord channels.
* Enforcing server security via 2FA verification and SeAT registration checks.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Your data is yours. **The operator will never sell, trade, or otherwise distribute your personal data to third parties.**

* **Legal Compliance:** The only exception to this rule is if the data is explicitly requested by law enforcement or government authorities under a valid legal mandate (such as a court order). As a Belgian citizen hosting data in Germany, the operator will comply with verified legal requests from relevant EU authorities.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored on a securely configured private server hosted by Contabo in Germany. While reasonable security measures are in place to protect the database and stored OAuth tokens, no system is entirely impenetrable. As stated in the Terms of Service, the operator assumes no liability for data breaches or data loss outside of reasonable control.

## 6. Data Retention and Your Rights

Data regarding character mappings and server configurations is kept as long as the Bot remains in the Discord server and the user remains mapped. Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

* **Access:** Request a copy of the data the Bot holds about you (via the `/char_info` command or by contacting the operator).
* **Erasure:** Have your data deleted. Removing your mapping (e.g., via `/rm_char_id`) or leaving the Discord server will result in the automated pruning of your data from the active database.
